What is D3?

D3 = Data-Driven Documents. It is a JavaScript library, but not a charting tool: there is no bar() button. D3 connects your data to elements on the page. One row of data → one shape (rect, circle, line, text). You use the data to decide size, position and colour. A bar chart is simply rectangles whose heights come from your numbers.

D3 vs. what you already know

D3 needs more code, and that is the trade-off: total control. The goal is not to replace Power BI, it is to understand visualization deeply and build what other tools can’t.
